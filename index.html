Turn Your Legacy Data into Exploration Targets

Unlock the Value of Your Exploration History

Your existing project data is an asset, one that you’ve already invested significant capital to collect. We help you leverage that knowledge to drive your current exploration program, ensuring you get the most ROI from your historical archives without stretching your internal resources.

Why partner with SJS for data compilation?

• Predictable Budgeting: By outsourcing your data backlog, you gain a fixed-cost solution for data management, allowing you to allocate your exploration budget more effectively.

• Operational Focus: Your team’s primary objective is to explore, not to spend weeks digitizing reports. Outsourcing allows your geologists to focus on what they do best: logging, mapping, and drill planning.

• Decision-Ready Deliverables: You receive clean, structured databases and high-quality visualizations designed to support both your internal technical planning and your external investor communications.

Historical Data Compilation Services

1. Legacy Data Migration

Converting multi-format historical records; maps, cross-sections, and assay tables-into modern, industry-standard GIS formats (GeoPackage/File Geodatabases).

2. Database Management & Integrity Audits

Structuring, cleaning, and validating legacy datasets. We ensure your database is free of formatting errors and ready for immediate ingestion into the modeling/GIS software of your choice.

3. Integrated Geospatial Compilation

Synthesizing disparate data streams- geochemical, drilling, mapping, and regional geophysics- into a unified spatial project, giving you a complete view of your land package.

4. High-Impact Visual Communication

Production of professional, publication-ready maps, 3D models, and interpretive diagrams that clearly communicate the value of your project to stakeholders and investors.

Stuart Stahl | Geological Data Management Consultant

I provide geological data management, remote sensing, and geospatial services for mineral exploration projects. My focus is on bridging the gap between historical paper archives and modern digital workflows.

I specialize in the end-to-end management of exploration data-from digitizing legacy maps and logs to building standardized, queryable databases. I design these database structures according to recognized industry standards, such as GeMS (Geologic Map Schema), to ensure your project data maintains interoperability and longevity.

My technical approach is built on a foundation of hands-on experience in active drilling and sampling programs. I’ve seen firsthand how much time is lost to data compilation projects that aren't properly formatted or topologically validated at the source. I build my data structures to eliminate that rework from the start.

I leverage Python and AI-assisted pipelines to automate high-volume data extraction where appropriate. By accelerating the repetitive stages of digitization, I can dedicate more time to the critical aspects of data management and the rigorous quality control that machines cannot yet replicate.

I’m here to manage the data pipeline so your team can focus on exploration.
